# Sponsorship model

Sponsorship is a bounded policy decision, not general relayer access. Limits will apply globally, per wallet, per action, and per transaction. Reservations prevent concurrent requests from exceeding exposure; low relayer balance and emergency-pause states fail closed.

Quotes must distinguish network/sponsor-cost reimbursement from any GASLESS service fee. Gas estimation is capped and revalidated, and actual settlement is reconciled without treating reimbursement as revenue.

No sponsor or relayer is configured in this scaffold.
